Featured Application

SustainAI — Smarter Sustainable Choices, Powered by AI

Live Demo GitHub Profile

SustainAI is what I built for my Green Skills & Applied AI internship — a Flask app that tries to make sustainability decisions a little less abstract. GreenRoute AI is the core of it: pick a route, and it works out the CO2 cost, a Green Score, and what you'd save by choosing differently. The AI Coach and food donation guidance were added once that core was solid.
